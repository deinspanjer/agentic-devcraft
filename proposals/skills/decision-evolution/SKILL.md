# Decision evolution

For each material decision, keep its basis and affected downstream conclusions
visible. When later evidence changes that basis:

1. Record the changed fact or constraint.
2. Mark the earlier decision superseded rather than overwriting it.
3. Record the replacement decision and why it follows from the new basis.
4. Revisit every downstream conclusion that depended on the superseded decision.

Preserve the user's chosen workflow and artifact format. Complete the update
only when the replacement and every affected downstream conclusion are current
and the supersession chain remains inspectable.
